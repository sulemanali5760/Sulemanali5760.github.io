About

I work on the control side of industrial systems: programming PLCs, designing and documenting control panels in EPLAN Electric P8, and commissioning low-voltage equipment. I studied electrical engineering and I am currently doing my master's at Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg.

What I enjoy most is the part where a design meets reality — the measurement that does not match the spec, and the work of finding out why. Most of the projects below started from exactly that.

Projects

OPC UA Test Automation Pipeline

Pulls live process data off a PLC over OPC UA, checks it against a written test-case spec, and generates a pass/fail report. Built around a real fault: a chattering contactor on a high-current feeder, traced to control voltage sagging to ~18 V against a 20 V hold-in spec under inrush.

Python · asyncua · OPC UA · YAML

TwinCAT 3 Pick-&-Place Framework

A minimal node-tree automation framework in Structured Text: one Cyclic() call on the root runs the whole machine, one SetMode() pushes the operating mode down the tree. Includes a pick-and-place unit that runs on a soft-PLC with no I/O attached.

TwinCAT 3 · Structured Text · IEC 61131-3

IoT Sensor Data Pipeline
